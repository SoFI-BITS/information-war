## Company profiles (participant-facing)

### NVTX — Novatex Devices
*Consumer technology · start price 142 · volatility: Very high*

Smartphones, wearables and home devices. Relies on imported components; launches a flagship handset this season.

**Known links:** Buys components from Forgeline. Sensitive to interest rates (growth stock).

### HBFG — Harbourline Financial Group
*Banking and financial services · start price 88 · volatility: High*

Valdoria's third-largest bank: retail deposits, business lending and a trading desk.

**Known links:** Earns more when interest rates rise. Lends to Solaris Ridge and Tidewell.

### PTRL — Petralis Energy
*Energy · start price 74 · volatility: High*

Oil and gas producer with offshore fields and a fuel distribution arm.

**Known links:** Competes with renewables for energy policy. Exports priced in foreign currency.

### CLVH — Calvera Health
*Healthcare · start price 126 · volatility: Very high*

Pharmaceutical developer. Its lead drug VX-7 (arthritis) is in late-stage trials.

**Known links:** Value depends heavily on VX-7 regulatory outcome and competitors.

### MRLW — Marlowe & Kent
*Retail and consumer goods · start price 64 · volatility: High*

Department stores and online retail. Holiday season is half of annual profit.

**Known links:** Imports most holiday stock through the Port of Ashmouth. Sensitive to consumer spending.

### FRGE — Forgeline Industries
*Industrial manufacturing · start price 96 · volatility: High*

Precision components: electronics parts, gearboxes and industrial bearings.

**Known links:** Supplies Novatex and the wind-turbine industry. Depends on imported bearings.

### TDWL — Tidewell Logistics
*Logistics and shipping · start price 68 · volatility: High*

Container terminals, trucking and Valdoria's only inland rail-freight corridor.

**Known links:** Fuel is a major cost. Moves goods for Marlowe & Kent and Greenfield.

### ARCM — Arcwave Communications
*Telecommunications · start price 82 · volatility: High*

Mobile and broadband network operator with high debt from its 5G build-out.

**Known links:** Debt-heavy, so sensitive to interest rates. Bidding in the 6G spectrum auction.

### GRNF — Greenfield Harvest Foods
*Agriculture and food · start price 61 · volatility: High*

Grain, dairy and packaged foods. Exports a large share of its output.

**Known links:** Exports via the Port of Ashmouth. Harvest yields drive annual results.

### SLRD — Solaris Ridge Renewables
*Renewable infrastructure · start price 110 · volatility: Very high*

Builds and operates solar and wind farms financed with long-term borrowing.

**Known links:** Depends on government subsidy policy and interest rates. Buys gearboxes from Forgeline.

## Volatility disclosure

All ten stocks are **high-volatility**. Typical round-to-round reference-value moves are within ±10%; 'Very high' stocks can move ±20% on a major event. This is disclosed to participants; the direction and timing of moves are not.
